Fresh Fruits, Better Choices

Garden Fresh makes it easier to explore fruits, compare prices, and discover fruit listings from sellers in one place.

Who We Are

Garden Fresh is a university project that demonstrates a simple online fruit marketplace using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Buyers can browse fruit listings, compare prices, select quantities, and manage their cart. Sellers can submit fruit information through the selling page.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

To make browsing fruit listings and comparing prices simple and convenient.

How Buying Works

  1. Open the Buy Fruits page.
  2. Search for a fruit or filter by price.
  3. Select the quantity you want.
  4. Add the fruit to your cart.
  5. Review your selected fruits and total price.

How Selling Works

  1. Open the Sell Fruits page.
  2. Enter the fruit and seller information.
  3. Choose a fruit picture if the form supports it.
  4. Complete the required fields and submit the form.

Seller listings are handled by the website's current front-end implementation. Permanent storage requires a database or another storage service.
